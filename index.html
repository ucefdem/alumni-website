about us

First One

Alumly is the alumni association of Lymed, founded in 2023 by a group of passionate and dedicated graduates. Created with the aim of supporting current students and strengthening connections among alumni, it has grown into a true network of solidarity, experience sharing, and mutual support.

Our Mission

01

Support

Current students in their preparation by providing academic and moral support, guidance, motivation and mentorship.

02

Maintain

The strong bonds between the alumni, and develop a symbiotical, dynamic, evolving, inclusive, and supportive network.

03

Collaborate

On various projects and events that unite, inspire, empower, engage, and strengthen the Lymed community.
